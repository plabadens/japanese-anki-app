Dev reference — pitch diagram component

Not part of the app UI. This documents the two scales and four pattern hues used everywhere pitch appears (review card, Edit word sheet).

Compact scale — inline in a review-card row
飲む — atamadaka ①. Mora 1 high (filled dot), mora 2 low (outline dot), no kana underneath — the vocabulary’s own ruby already shows its lemma reading. Reused verbatim on the review card's vocab row.
Full scale — Edit word sheet
飲む — atamadaka ①. Same word, full scale: bordered block per mora, kana printed under its own dot. No pattern name, no accent number — the reading is the only label.
上手 — atamadaka ① (one of two listed patterns for this spelling).
上手 — heiban ⓪, the other listed pattern. Both appear together in the Edit word sheet's pick-list (screen 23); which is active uses a neutral outline and check, not pitch color alone.
Pattern-color reference only — not a looked-up word
Nakadaka hue (drop somewhere in the middle) — preserve every mora; a long diagram scrolls horizontally instead of shrinking kana or truncating the word.
Odaka hue — a short trailing drop marks the word boundary, unlike heiban’s continuing high contour. The tail is not another mora or a prediction of sentence intonation.
