What this layout is

The bar holds nothing but the mode switch, centred on the same axis as the shutter, and the settings gear tucked right. No screen title: the selected segment is the identity of the screen. The viewfinder is the uncropped 4:3 sensor shown upright, full width. Below it, the shutter row sits on whitespace that is exactly equal above and below, so it lands about 120 dp from the bottom edge — inside the thumb arc — instead of jammed against the bezel.

The right slot is a torch, not a flash. A document scanner wants continuous light: you see the reflection in the viewfinder and can tilt until the paper stops glaring, which a burst at the moment of capture cannot do. It is a toggle with a persistent state for the session, and it goes off when you leave Capture.
